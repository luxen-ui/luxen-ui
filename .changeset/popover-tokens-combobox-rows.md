Panels anchored to a trigger and their rows now share new `--l-popover-*` and `--l-menu-item-*` design tokens, so dropdowns, popovers, selects and comboboxes look the same and can be re-themed in one place. Combobox options now match dropdown items: 36px rows, 14px text and a hover highlight. In both `<l-select>` and `<l-combobox>`, the mouse and the keyboard share a single highlighted option. `--show-duration` and `--hide-duration` now also accept values in seconds. A combobox opened from its chevron now closes when you click outside it.
