`<l-drawer>` gains a `top` placement and an `inset` attribute. `inset` detaches the drawer from the viewport edges, floating it with a uniform gap (`--inset-gap`, default `1rem`), rounded corners on every side, and a drop shadow (`--shadow`). Both work with every `placement` (`start`, `end`, `top`, `bottom`).
