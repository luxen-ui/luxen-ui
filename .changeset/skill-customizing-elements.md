The skill generated by `luxen-ui generate-skill` now covers customizing an element, not only emitting one. Agents are told to read an element's spec before styling it or working around one of its behaviours, and to tune it through its documented custom properties, parts and events — and, when the knob does not exist, to report the gap upstream rather than ship a local workaround.
